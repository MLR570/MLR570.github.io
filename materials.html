Past projects

Final projects

The final project is done in teams of three and is due in week 15. Both of these started as student work and ended up published.

PROJECT

Class Separation Dynamics in Vision Transformers: An Empirical Study

Dara Varam, Lujain Khalil, Mahmoud Darwish and Mohamed I. AlHajri. Tracks how far apart a vision transformer holds its classes at every layer, and finds that the separation spikes at the first transformer block and then decays exponentially with depth.

IEEE Access · vol. 13, 2025, pp. 153651-153670
PROJECT

Different Strokes for Different Folks: Writer Identification for Historical Arabic Manuscripts

Hamza A. Abushahla, Ariel Justine N. Panopio, Layth Al-Khairulla and Mohamed I. AlHajri. Identifies the scribe behind a historical Arabic manuscript from the handwriting itself.

IJDAR · 2026

Code for the vision transformer study is on GitHub.

Past surveys

Survey reports

The survey report is a separate, individual assignment, due in week 10. You pick a topic area and write a detailed survey of it from a literature review. Both assignments are described on the syllabus.

SURVEY

Neural Network Quantization for Microcontrollers: A Comprehensive Survey of Methods, Platforms, and Applications

Hamza A. Abushahla, Dara Varam, Ariel Justine N. Panopio and Mohamed I. AlHajri. Covers the quantization methods used to fit neural networks onto microcontrollers, the hardware and software platforms that run them, and where the accuracy goes.

arXiv · 2508.15008, 2025
