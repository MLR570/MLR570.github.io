The idea

A living, breathing companion to the course.

This site is meant to be used, not just read. Every definition, property list and worked example is here, and next to them are examples you can change and re-run. Drag a vector, edit a matrix, move a slider, and the numbers update as you go.

Drag the actual objects

Grab the columns of a matrix and watch the determinant, the rank and the eigenvectors respond in real time. The intuition arrives before the algebra does.

Break things on purpose

Push the learning rate until gradient descent explodes. Collapse a matrix until it stops being invertible. The failure modes are where the understanding is.

Verified, line by line

Every worked example on this site has been recomputed and checked, and the figures compute their results live rather than showing a saved answer.

The notes

Six chapters, built in order.

Each chapter assumes the ones before it. Chapter 1 is the mathematical toolkit the rest of the course stands on, and Chapter 2 covers everything you do to data before and after a model sees it. The later chapters are listed here so you can see where the semester is going, but they stay closed until we reach them in class.

CHAPTER 1

Mathematical Background

The linear algebra, probability and statistics, and calculus and optimization that the rest of the course assumes you already have.

Live 26 interactive figures · 3 parts
CHAPTER 2

Data Fundamentals

Preprocessing and feature engineering, similarity and dissimilarity measures, and how to evaluate a model honestly.

Live 21 interactive figures · 3 parts
CHAPTER 3

Supervised Learning I

Linear classification and the perceptron, support vector machines, regularized least squares, kernel methods and k-nearest neighbors.

Not yet released 10 interactive figures · 5 parts
CHAPTER 4

Trees & Neural Networks

Decision trees with entropy, information gain and pruning, then feedforward neural networks, activation functions and backpropagation.

Not yet released 12 interactive figures · 2 parts
CHAPTER 5

CNNs, RNNs & Transformers

Convolutional networks for images, recurrent networks for sequences, and the attention mechanism behind transformers.

Not yet released 21 interactive figures · 3 parts
CHAPTER 6

Unsupervised Learning

Clustering with k-means, k-medoids, DBSCAN and Gaussian mixtures, plus autoencoders for representation learning.

How to use this

Suggestions, not rules.

If you are taking the course

Read the section, then spend two minutes in the playground beneath it before moving on. Try to predict what will happen before you drag anything.

If you are here to revise

Use the sidebar to jump straight to a topic. Every definition, property list and worked example from the original notes is here, so nothing you revised from before has moved.
